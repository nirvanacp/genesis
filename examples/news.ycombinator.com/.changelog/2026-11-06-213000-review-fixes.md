# Category reads and page reloads

- Goal: Fix the review findings for Hacker News: Saved reread its categories on every render, and Me and Post forced fresh pages with URL stamps and a reload intent.
- Changes: `useLibrary` depends on the memoized category repository, so categories load once and then only on a category change. Me reloads its page with `useNavigation().reload` on resume and after sign-out, and Post another reloads the submit form the same way. The `me.reload` intent and the URL stamps are gone.
- Decisions: None new; `reload()` is the SDK's refresh operation.
- Verification: `typecheck` and `check` passed. Device recheck results are in the platform record of this change.
