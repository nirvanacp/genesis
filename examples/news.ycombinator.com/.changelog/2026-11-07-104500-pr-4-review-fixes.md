# Post sign-in, link errors and the submit notice

- Goal: Fix the Hacker News findings of the PR 4 review: Post stayed signed out after signing in through the website sheet, a link the platform could not open failed silently, and the notice after submitting said the post was sent.
- Changes: Post reloads its form on resume while it shows the sign-in prompt. `useOpenRoute` logs a failed open instead of dropping it. The notice reads "Submitted to Hacker News". The SDK now asks the Post leave guard before tab or link navigation too, and the platform opens `http://` story links in the Android browser.
- Decisions: Post reloads only while signed out, so returning to the app does not hide a form in progress behind a skeleton. Agent choice.
- Verification: `typecheck` and `check` passed; `dev install` installed revision 13 on `emulator-5554` with the applet in front. On the device, Post showed sign-in for a signed-in account because HN changed its submit form's `action` (recorded in the current spec), so the resume reload, the guarded tab tap and posting were not exercised there. No HN action was performed.
