# Adapter runtime vertical slice

- Goal: Move Hacker News to manifest schema 6 and the adapter runtime for Home, discussions and profiles (step 7 of the session projection migration).
- Changes: One `primary` context and routes `home`, `news`, `item` and `user`. A new adapter with `feed`, `item` and `user` pages replaces `src/sources`. The screens read `usePage`, `useRegion` and `useRecord`, rows are `memo` components that read `usePending`, and votes use the `setVoted` action. Reply, comment and sign-in open HN's pages through `navigate({ url })`. The other tabs are disabled placeholders and their screens are left out of the build. Adapter tests run with `npm test` on `linkedom`.
- Decisions: decision-0005.
- Verification: `check`, `build`, `build --debug`, `typecheck`, `npm test` and `check-packages` passed, and the bundle imports as Current. Nothing was run on a device; no HN action was performed.
