# Show region read errors

- Goal: Tell the user when part of a screen couldn't be read, instead of leaving a section out without saying so.
- Changes: Explore, the feeds, Profile and Post show an inline "couldn't be read" note when one of their regions reports a read error. When nothing else is on the screen, the note replaces the empty notice or the skeleton.
- Verification: `typecheck` and `genesis-applet check` passed. On the emulator, a temporary `throw` in `readTrend` showed "Some results couldn't be read." above People on Explore, and the throw was then removed. No X action was performed.
