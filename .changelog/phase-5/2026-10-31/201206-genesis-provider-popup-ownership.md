# Provider popup and callback ownership

- Goal: Complete ordinary foreground link behavior and prevent hidden source documents from requesting visible provider UI.
- Changes: User-activated target-blank links in an owned foreground now use normal native internal/provider routing. Transient popup WebViews are muted, bounded to two, expire after five seconds, reject stale ownership and are destroyed on visit teardown or renderer failure. File picker and fullscreen callbacks require the exact visible reader, including callback completion identity. Hidden sources and transient popups cannot borrow another reader's visible privileges. Source navigation is deferred while an independent reader is visible; exact-current source snapshots still update. Close revalidates changed/unready sources or recreates a failed foreground. Late owned navigation cannot replace the reader, pending Back tokens are canceled, and callbacks after teardown are ignored. Provider sessions have unique IDs; stale Close IDs cannot close newer readers. Failed foregrounds cannot bind again before Retry.
- Decisions: The agent found that the previous global website flag admitted any source/external view, which was too broad. Normal web links are supported without promising general opener-dependent authentication popup compatibility.
- Verification: Android assembly and lint passed for the separate QA host. A disposable API-3 foreground with a plain target-blank anchor opened the real external origin in the bridge-free reader; Close restored the owned opener. A second probe changed hidden source history while the external origin stayed correct; Close restored a freshly validated owned opener. The temporary reducer verifier passed both actual manifests, including independent publication, navigation deferral, reader replacement rejection and failed-foreground return. The fixture stayed out of everyday Genesis; its separate host is removed during final cleanup. No repository test files changed.
