# Choose your first idea

[Learning path](../README.md) · Step 2

Start with a website you already use and one change you can see immediately.

## What can you create?

These are examples of app ideas, not a catalog of ready-made apps.

| Idea | What you ask for | What it adds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A calmer news reader | Larger text, clearer spacing, fewer distracting blocks | A website layout change |
| A personal research library | Save links with notes and categories | Custom controls, a Saved page, and stored records |
| A reading app across two sites | Collect stories from both sites in one library | Several sources sharing app data |
| A custom website client | Keep browsing, search, profiles, and account flows; add your own navigation | A broader app with several journeys to check |
| A collection available on several devices | Store records in a service you operate | A separate server, account rules, and backups |

## A good first project

> Make Hacker News easier for me to read. Increase story text size and spacing. Keep the story links, comments, sign-in, and voting behavior working.

You can tell whether this worked by opening a story and coming back. It needs no separate server.

After that, add a Saved screen if you want one. Decide where its data should live before building it.

## Make the boundary clear

Tell your agent whether you want a full website client or a focused tool.

For example, "Keep the full social client and add categories to Saved" means one app with its existing browsing and account flows. It does not mean a separate categories-only app.

## Ideas that need more planning

Continuous background collection, a server running inside the phone, and full offline copies of websites are not current Genesis capabilities.

Website permissions, sign-in challenges, and changing page layouts can also limit an idea. Ask your agent to identify those limits before promising the result.
