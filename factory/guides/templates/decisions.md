# Decisions

Append material choices here; current behavior belongs in `current.md`. Use stable ids and real local dates.

## decision-0001: Decision title

- Date and origin: local date; a user instruction, inherited evidence or an agent choice.
- Choice and rationale: the exact behavior, the reason, its scope, and the rules and journeys it affects.
- Evidence and supersession: the supporting file or observation, and the decision ids it replaces, or none.
